centered on the Glamis Sand Dunes Recreation Area (Imperial County, CA / AZ border region):
Glamis Dunes Rentals, Gilmore Offroad, Sand Hollow, Polaris Adventures, BRP Experiences all
operate exactly this business model at this exact location. "ThrillOffroad" is a plausible,
non-colliding brand name for this real business type.

Built as a non-CCA-branded, non-insurance lead-gen site: **ThrillOffroad — UTV/SXS rental &
guided dune-tour outfitter serving the Glamis Sand Dunes Recreation Area.** Not CCA-affiliated
— no CCA phone/address/branding used (canonical CCA contact info is insurance-specific and
doesn't apply to this build).
